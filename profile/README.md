# Veracto

Veracto helps companies, researchers and research teams identify useful applications of AI and put them into practice.

Our work starts with the problem: understanding the task, evaluating where AI can help, and designing an approach that fits the people and work involved. We focus on consulting and support for adoption.

- [Website case study](https://github.com/timsmykov/veracto-site-showcase) — an independent public edition of the bilingual site, published by Veracto co-founder Tim Smykov.
